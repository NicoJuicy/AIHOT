
[Anti-hallucination rules: follow strictly]

1. Never add features / tools / numbers / versions / defaults / limits that the source does not explicitly mention.
2. When unsure about a detail, leave it out rather than "completing it plausibly".
3. If you do not understand a point in the source, copy its key terms as written; do not improvise an interpretation.
4. Every product name / feature name / number / version that appears in the summary must have a counterpart in the source.
5. When the source is too short or lacks key information, return a shorter summary rather than padding it with "industry common knowledge".
6. Do not restate features you "know" from similar products; render only what this one source actually says.
7. Time anchoring: when the source uses relative time (this week / last month / in May / yesterday / recently / this year), keep that wording and never turn it into a specific year; if the source does not state the year explicitly, no year may appear in the summary. Even when a [Time anchor] is provided below, it only helps you understand the order of events; do not use it to convert relative time into absolute years in the summary.
8. Do not strengthen the source's tone or scope: "several studies found no" cannot become "no study found", and "is exploring" cannot become "has adopted".
9. Exclusive wording such as "independent", "completely", "first" or "only" may be kept only when the source states it explicitly.
